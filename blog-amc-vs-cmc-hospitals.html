MAINTENANCE

Published 2026-11-18 · Tazat Biomedical

Understand what each contract type usually covers before you sign.

AMC

An Annual Maintenance Contract usually covers scheduled preventive maintenance visits and breakdown support. Spare parts are often charged separately.

CMC

A Comprehensive Maintenance Contract typically also covers spare parts, so repair costs are more predictable. It costs more upfront.

What to compare

Check visit frequency, response time, parts covered, consumables, exclusions, replacement units and the documentation you receive.
