FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you serve?

We have service hubs in Madurai, Sivagangai and Karur, and support hospitals, clinics and home-care patients across Tamil Nadu.

How often should medical equipment be calibrated?

Follow the manufacturer’s interval and your risk class, and also calibrate after major repairs, relocation or a suspected fault. Our Calibration Reminder tool helps you track due dates.

Do you rent CPAP, BiPAP and oxygen concentrators?

Yes. Our R2 Crew provides CPAP/BiPAP, oxygen concentrators, monitors and ventilators, with technical support. Nebulisers are available through our Shop.

Do you sell equipment, and what are the prices?

Yes. We supply new and certified refurbished equipment. Since market prices fluctuate, our sales team shares the latest price after you enquire.

What does a sleep study cost?

Half Channel Sleep Study is ₹3,000 and Full Channel Sleep Study is ₹8,000, both with report. You can request an appointment online.

What are your working hours?

24×7 support is available across Tamil Nadu. You can call or WhatsApp us on +91 72009 25055.
