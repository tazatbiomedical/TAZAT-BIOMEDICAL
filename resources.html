Standard Calibration Frequency Guide

General recommendations by clinical risk category.

EquipmentRisk classRecommended cycleCritical parameters
ICU VentilatorHigh (Life Support)Every 6 MonthsTidal volume, PEEP, FiO₂, flow sensor
DefibrillatorHigh (Emergency)Every 6 MonthsDelivered energy (J), sync time
Infusion / Syringe PumpMedium-HighAnnual (6–12 Mo)Flow accuracy, occlusion pressure
Anaesthesia MachineHigh (Surgical)Every 6 MonthsVaporizer output, hypoxic guard, pressure
Patient Monitor (ECG/SpO₂)MediumEvery 12 MonthsLead rhythm, SpO₂ curve, NIBP
Infant Radiant WarmerHigh (Neonatal)Every 6 MonthsProbe accuracy, irradiance, alarms
